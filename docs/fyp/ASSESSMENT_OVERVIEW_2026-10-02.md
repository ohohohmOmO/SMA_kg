## Reporting implications

- The preliminary report must make every objective SMART and include a clear
  Gantt chart; both are explicit grading checks. The current draft page 8 has a
  schedule table and needs an actual Gantt chart.
- The interim report must demonstrate genuine technical progress, literature
  coverage, initiative, and a feasible revised plan rather than only describe
  activity.
- Continuous performance rewards documented planning and independent problem
  solving. Logbooks, meeting records, decisions, validation evidence, and
  reproducible run artifacts should therefore be maintained throughout the
  project.
- Oral presentation marks are concentrated in technical content and answers
  to questions under the separate sheet. The handbook also assesses the poster;
  prepare both, as well as the independent interim presentation.
- Under the separate final sheet, marks concentrate on technical content and
  analysis, which carries three times the weighting of each other category.
  Claims about the four planned innovation modules must remain aligned with
  their verified implementation and evaluation status.
