## Confirmed assessment weights

| Assessment | Weight | Main criteria |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Project Specifications and Preliminary Report | 5% | Project description; tasks and targets; SMART objectives; technical background; Gantt chart |
| Interim Report | 15% | Technical background; problem details; literature review; initiative; feasible revised work plan |
| Quality of Technical Work and Continuous Performance | 10% | Planning (1); initiative (1); professional conduct (1); technical quality of work (2) |
| Oral Presentation | 20% | Delivery (1); slides (1); technical content (2); structure (1); responses to questions (2) |
| Final Report | 50% | Writing (1); presentation and figures (1); organisation and structure (1); literature survey (1); technical content and quality of analysis (3) |

The five weights total 100%.

## Reporting implications

- The preliminary report must make every objective SMART and include a clear
  Gantt chart; both are explicit grading checks.
- The interim report must demonstrate genuine technical progress, literature
  coverage, initiative, and a feasible revised plan rather than only describe
  activity.
- Continuous performance rewards documented planning and independent problem
  solving. Logbooks, meeting records, decisions, validation evidence, and
  reproducible run artifacts should therefore be maintained throughout the
  project.
- Oral presentation marks are concentrated in technical content and answers
  to questions. These two categories have twice the weighting of delivery,
  slides, or structure.
- Final report marks are concentrated in technical content and quality of
  analysis, which carries three times the weighting of each other category.
  Claims about the four planned innovation modules must remain aligned with
  their verified implementation and evaluation status.
